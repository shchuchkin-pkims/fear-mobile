v0.5.0 – Handles, contacts and personal chats

The relay gains state: registered handles, an encrypted contact list
per user, and personal chats that work without the other side online.
Completes Phase B of the roadmap.

Added:
- Handle registration (nickname@server) on first connect; one key has
  one handle per server. A restored identity finds its handle again by
  its key.
- An encrypted contact list kept on the relay and synchronised; the
  relay cannot read it.
- Personal chats under a key derived from both identities (X25519):
  opening one does not need the other person online.
- Encrypted identity backup as a .fbk file or a password-protected QR
  code, imported from the camera or an image.
- A peer profile on tapping an avatar or a name; removing a contact or
  a room with a long press.
- Local message history with full-text search.
- The connect mode is chosen automatically: an empty room is created
  with a fresh key, a populated one is joined by key exchange.
- Application keep-alive: a ping every 60 seconds; the relay closes a
  connection silent for 4 minutes.

Changed:
- One sidebar with Contacts and Groups; "+" adds a contact, joins a
  room or creates one.
- The participant list opens from the chat header and shows only
  members who are present.
- Light and dark themes; the choice survives restarts.

Fixed:
- Reconnection after the device leaves Doze.

Known limitations:
- No forward secrecy; scheduled for Phase C.

Requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer.
