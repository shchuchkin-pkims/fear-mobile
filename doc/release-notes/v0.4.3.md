v0.4.3 – Video call reliability and RTT display

Video calls recover from errors faster, and every call shows its
round-trip time.

Added:
- Round-trip time during voice and video calls: green under 100 ms,
  yellow 100–300 ms, red above.

Changed:
- Medium video quality: 800 kbit/s instead of 500.
- A keyframe every second instead of every two, for faster recovery.

Fixed:
- A race in the HELLO handshake timed the call out when the peer's
  HELLO arrived first.
- Over the TCP relay every VP8 frame is decoded in order; frames are
  skipped to the next keyframe only when the backlog grows too large.
- The VP8 decoder is recreated after an internal error, and P-frames
  are skipped until the next keyframe, so the picture is not garbled.
