v0.6.0 – Key rotation, metadata privacy, group calls

Android catches up with the desktop on Phases C to F: the room key
follows the room, the relay no longer learns room names or display
names, calls work for groups, and messages reach contacts who are
offline.

Added:
- Room key rotation on every change of membership, byte for byte the
  same as on the desktop (pinned by shared test vectors). A newcomer
  cannot read what came before; a member who left cannot read what
  comes after.
- Group voice (a decoder per participant, mixed) and group video (one
  person large, the others in a strip, names under the tiles).
- An incoming call screen; a video call can be answered from the
  invitation; a call stays up while unanswered.
- Offline inbox: mail for a contact who is offline waits on the relay,
  sealed, and is collected even while you are in another room.
- Video adapts to the network: the bitrate drops when the upload
  cannot keep up and returns to your setting when it clears; the frame
  rate setting is a real limit; CBR where the encoder supports it.
- Microphone sensitivity; the system noise suppressor can be switched
  off where it cuts quiet speech.
- The member count of a room; a date above each day; unread counts in
  the chat list.

Changed:
- The relay sees a hash instead of the room name and a random tag per
  connection instead of your name.
- Chat sealed under per-epoch keys; calls with a key per sender and a
  replay window per sender.
- One fingerprint per key on every platform; trusted keys saved under
  the old short form are renamed and keep their "verified" mark.
- The background service also checks the offline inbox; notifications
  still need nothing from Google.
- License: GPL-3.0-or-later instead of MIT; the text ships in the APK.

Fixed:
- The database no longer wipes the history on a schema change.
- Leaving a video call no longer kills the app, which skipped the key
  wiping.

Security:
- Key delivery requires a verified signature; no key material in logs.
- Contact lists on the relay can be read only by their owner (a signed
  one-shot challenge).
- The lock screen shows "New message" – no text, no sender.

Known limitations:
- No post-compromise security (no ratchet).
- Notifications cannot wake an app the system has killed: that takes
  Firebase, which would give Google the timing of your messages.
- Direct calls (STUN) are desktop-only for now.
